## About Me

I studied computer science at **Livingstone College**, graduating in May 2026 with a 4.00
GPA, and spent three summers building production software at **Amazon Web Services**,
**Bank of America**, and **Ernst &amp; Young**.

What holds my work together is a preference for systems that give people more capability
rather than more dependency. The AI tutor I build refuses to hand over answers. The
task-initiation tool I build is designed to need less of a student's attention, not more.
That is a deliberate stance, and it decides what I choose to build.

My work sits at the intersection of:

* Agentic AI and multi-agent architectures
* Cloud and serverless systems on AWS
* Full-stack product engineering
* Accessibility and technology for underserved communities
* Applied machine learning

## What I Am Building

### Spiritus Agentic Solutions

**Autonomous AI agents, built for African realities.**

Spiritus is the studio my product work ships under. The constraint that shapes everything
there is that intelligence has to survive contact with an unreliable network, a shared
device, and a user who is not working in English.

Current areas of work:

* Conversational agents that stay useful over text-first, low-bandwidth connections
* Retrieval grounded in a user's own material rather than a general corpus
* Multilingual interfaces where the concept lands first and the English term follows
* Adaptive systems that respond to behaviour instead of waiting to be configured

## Research Direction

1. How does an agent stay useful when the network is unreliable, the device is shared, and
   the user is not working in English?

2. What does an AI tutor owe a learner? Handing over the answer is the easy path and the
   wrong one, so what does refusing well actually look like?

3. How should a system infer that someone is stuck without surveilling what they write?

4. How do you keep an agentic pipeline explainable enough that a regulated organisation
   will put it in front of its own staff?

5. When is a small, interpretable model the better answer than a large general one?
